The long page

This page exists to be scrolled beside a browser. Every section below is numbered and anchored, so a section that looks wrong can be named: the heading of section forty-one is #s41. Each section leads with a different construct and they cycle, so a screenful anywhere on the page has something worth comparing on it.

What is expected to differ, and is not a bug: LazInk takes its colors from the control's theme rather than the page's, it draws no form controls, nothing floats or flows around a picture, and a line holding a padded pill grows to fit it rather than letting the pill overlap its neighbors the way a browser does. Everything else should line up.
